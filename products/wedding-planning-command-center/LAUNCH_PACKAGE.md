# Wedding Planning Command Center — Launch Package

## Product positioning

A premium wedding-planning operating system for couples who want one place to control budget, deadlines, vendors, guests, payments, decisions, seating, and day-of execution.

This should not be positioned as "another wedding spreadsheet." The core promise is visibility and control: **Are we on budget? Are we on time? What needs attention?**

## Deliverables

- Excel workbook with Start Here, Dashboard, Tasks, Budget, Guests, Vendors, Payments, Seating, Decisions, and Day Of tabs
- 66 pre-built planning milestones tied to the wedding date
- Automated dashboard KPIs and attention flags
- Budget allocation and commitment tracking
- Vendor contract/payment tracking
- RSVP and seating support
- Decision log and day-of operations timeline
- Quick Start PDF
- Native Google Sheets edition maintained separately in private Google Drive

## Recommended launch price

**$29 introductory price**

Rationale: high enough to avoid competing as a commodity $3-$10 checklist, while still being low-friction for a new listing without reviews. Test upward after initial conversion evidence.

Suggested pricing tests after traction:

- $29 — launch / initial review-building price
- $39 — standard price if conversion remains healthy
- $49 — only after stronger proof, bundled bonuses, or meaningful product expansion

The first goal is not maximizing revenue. It is proving that strangers will pay for the product.

Track:

- listing visits
- checkout conversion
- first purchase date
- refund rate
- buyer questions
- which images/copy drive engagement
- price resistance

Do not add large new features until real buyer behavior identifies a need.
